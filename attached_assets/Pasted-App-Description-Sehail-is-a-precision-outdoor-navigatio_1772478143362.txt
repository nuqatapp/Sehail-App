App Description

Sehail is a precision outdoor navigation app built around clarity, direction, and cultural grounding. It is designed for users who move with purpose, whether exploring open terrain, navigating urban environments, or tracking orientation in unfamiliar landscapes.

Core Concept

The identity is anchored in a four pointed guiding star with an elongated southern axis, integrating the letter S to reinforce directional focus. The symbol reflects guidance, stability, and intentional movement. The interface design follows the same philosophy, minimal, controlled, and highly legible.

Brand Color System

Primary Background
Saudi Flag Green
HEX #006C35
RGB 0, 108, 53

This solid green defines the app environment. It communicates authority, national identity, and trust. It is used across splash screens, onboarding flows, and primary UI backgrounds.

Primary Accent
Warm Gold
Recommended flat tone: #D4AF37

Gold represents guidance and premium positioning. It is used for the main logo, directional indicators, active navigation markers, and high priority UI elements. It must remain flat, without gradients or metallic effects.

Neutral System

White
#FFFFFF
Used for primary typography on green backgrounds, map labels, and clean interface overlays.

Black
#000000
Reserved for monochrome logo usage, system text on white layouts, and formal documentation.

Design Language

The interface avoids visual noise. No gradients, no shadows, no decorative effects. All elements are flat, sharp, and symmetrical. Typography remains modern and highly legible to ensure quick cognitive processing during movement.

Experience Positioning

Sehail is not a generic mapping tool. It positions itself as a directional authority rooted in Saudi identity while maintaining a globally minimal aesthetic. The Saudi Flag Green foundation ensures immediate cultural alignment. The gold star reinforces guidance and premium reliability. White and black maintain institutional flexibility across digital and print environments.

The result is a navigation product that signals precision, heritage, and controlled modernity.
